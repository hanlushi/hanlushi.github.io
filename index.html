About

I am a first-year PhD candidate at the University of Melbourne (from March 2026), supervised by Dr Jenny Waycott and supported by a Melbourne Research Scholarship. I am part of the ARC Industrial Transformation Training Centre for Optimal Ageing (ITTC Optimal Ageing), and my PhD is conducted with Amazon Alexa Australia as industry partner.

I also work as a Research Assistant at Deakin University's Institute for Physical Activity and Nutrition (IPAN), Faculty of Health, on digital health studies. Before my PhD I completed a Master of Information Technology at Monash University, after a Bachelor of Education, so I came to HCI from teaching and software development.

Current projects

  • PhD · University of Melbourne

    Voice assistants in the homes of older adults

    How older adults use, adapt and judge voice assistants in everyday life, and what this means for designing and evaluating AI for healthy ageing. Presenting at the OzCHI 2026 Doctoral Consortium.

  • Evaluation methods

    Evaluating LLM and RAG systems

    Using LLM-as-a-judge and multi-tier metrics to assess the quality, grounding and safety of language-model and retrieval-augmented systems.

  • Deakin IPAN

    CAPABLE-T2D

    A field study within the SMART programme exploring a digital, AI-supported lifestyle tool with continuous glucose monitoring for people with type 2 diabetes.

  • Design & build

    Home exercise and rehabilitation app

    A tablet app supporting home-based exercise and rehabilitation, built with React Native and a cloud back end.
