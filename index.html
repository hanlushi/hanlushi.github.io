More research · 4 projects

PhD / Landscape review

What can Alexa support?

A systematic review of approximately 9,700 Australian third-party Alexa skills, mapped to older adults’ functional needs.

Level 4 and 5 screening in progress.

The review maps skills to the WHO functional ability framework, Integrated Care for Older People (ICOPE), and activities of daily living (ADL/IADL). An expert panel checks the mapping using a light Delphi process.
